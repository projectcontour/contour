Add support for limiting the maximum request body size with `listener.max-request-body-bytes` in the Contour configuration file or `spec.envoy.listener.maxRequestBodyBytes` in the `ContourConfiguration` CRD. Requests with a larger body are rejected with 413. The limit applies to all routes and is not compatible with WebSocket or gRPC client-streaming and bidirectional streaming calls: it counts the total data the client sends over the connection or call, so long-lived streams are closed once the limit is reached, even if each message is small.
