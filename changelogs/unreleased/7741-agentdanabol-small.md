Add `http1SafeMaxConnectionDuration` field to the ContourConfiguration CRD and `http1-safe-max-connection-duration` to the Contour configuration file. When enabled alongside `maxConnectionDuration`, Envoy tears down downstream HTTP/1 connections in an RFC 9112 compliant way -- waiting for the next request, adding a `Connection: close` header to the response and closing once that stream has completed -- instead of starting a drain timer and closing the connection without signalling the client. Defaults to `false`, matching Envoy.
